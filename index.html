Research

I work on dynamical systems, particularly the topology of surface dynamics, rotation theory and the detection of chaos. A central goal of my research is to develop effective mathematical criteria for identifying complex dynamics in concrete systems arising in classical mechanics and other sciences.

My interests include annular and toral dynamics, rotational horseshoes, invariant continua, and the interplay between topological methods and computer-assisted proofs.
